# individual

One item per person the authors assert carries a variant in the gene of interest. Quote the passage that first describes them.

- **`case`**: the article's label, then sex, age and ethnicity as stated, for example "II-1, female, 34 years, of Finnish descent". Use the label exactly as the article writes it, because curators search by it.
- **`previouslyPublished`**: where the article says this person was reported before, with the PMID or reference it gives, for example "PMID:12345678" or "Smith et al. 2010 (reference 14)".
- **`supplementalData`**: the supplementary file, table or figure that reports more about them, as the article names it, for example "Supplementary Table S2".

Do not make individuals of:

- relatives without a variant in the gene, who belong in `case-family`;
- cohort members reported only in aggregate ("12 of 40 patients carried …"), who go under `notInBatch`.

If the article numbers individuals only in a table, quote the table row's text from its `TABLE` passage.
