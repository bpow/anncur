# case-testing

How the individual was tested. Quote the sentence that says it.

- **`genotypingMethod`**: how the variant was found or confirmed, for example "Exome sequencing", "Targeted gene panel (12 cardiomyopathy genes)", "Sanger sequencing of MYH7" or "Sanger confirmation".
- **`casePreviousTesting`**: testing done before the test that found the variant, in words:
  - earlier genetic tests and their results;
  - other genes tested and found negative;
  - the panel's name or its table of genes;
  - whether genome-wide methods were used;
  - whether the authors say no previous testing was done.

A methods paragraph that applies to every individual gives one fact naming them all.
