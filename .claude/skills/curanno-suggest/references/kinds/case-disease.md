# case-disease

The disease or diagnosis the authors assert for the individual. Quote the sentence that asserts it.

- **`diseaseAssertion`** (required): the diagnosis as the authors name it, for example "hypertrophic cardiomyopathy" or "left ventricular noncompaction". Add the MIM number or other identifier only if the article gives it.

If the authors assert no disease for an individual, make no fact. Do not name a disease that the phenotypes merely suggest.
