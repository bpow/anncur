# case-family

What the article says about the individual's family. Quote the sentence that says it.

- **`familyInfo`** (required), in a sentence or two:
  - affected relatives and whether they carry the variant;
  - unaffected carriers;
  - segregation;
  - consanguinity;
  - de novo status where the authors assert it;
  - the inheritance pattern the authors state;
  - the pedigree figure or table it refers to.

Record only what is asserted. "Family history was negative" is a fact worth recording. Silence about the family is not.
